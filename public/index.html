The ritual —
four steps to the summit

Great leaf deserves a slow moment. Follow the protocol our tasters use at base camp.

i.

Measure the leaf

One heaped teaspoon (≈ 2.5 g) per 200 ml. Never pack the spoon — whole leaf needs room to unfurl.

ii.

Mind the water

Black & dark: full boil, 95°C. Green & white: cool to 75°C — boiling water scorches the delicate buds.

iii.

Steep with patience

3 minutes for green, 4 for white, 5 for black. Set a timer. Walking away is part of the ritual.

iv.

Sip, don't gulp

Let it cool to 60°C. The first sip should land like cold mountain air — bright, clean, endless.
